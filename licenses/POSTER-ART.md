# Poster art

The poster artwork (`app/src/main/res/drawable/bezos_bg.png`, `website/assets/poster.png` and the social preview image derived from it) comes from [bezos.click](https://bezos.click).

PageDrop uses it with the artist's permission, for the app, the project website and press material about PageDrop.

This permission is not an open licence. The artwork is not covered by PageDrop's GPL-3.0 licence, and forks or other projects that want to reuse it must ask the artist themselves. Because of this, F-Droid builds declare the `NonFreeAssets` anti-feature until the artwork is released under a free licence (for example CC BY 4.0) or replaced.
